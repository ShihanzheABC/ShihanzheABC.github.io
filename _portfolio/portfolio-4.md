## Project Overview
This research focuses on near-infrared organic photodetectors (NIR OPDs) and organic electrochromic (EC) devices. An adaptive photodetector resistant to glare interference was developed by integrating a NIR OPD with a voltage-tunable EC device, thereby mimicking the light-adaptation behavior of human vision. A manuscript based on this work is currently in preparation and is expected to be completed by October.
